App screenshots for the "Published apps" cards.
Each card shows up to 3 portrait phone screenshots (about 300x640 px, JPG or WebP, under 60 KB each).
Add them to a project in js/projects.js, e.g.:
  images: ["assets/images/projects/app-1.jpg", "assets/images/projects/app-2.jpg", "assets/images/projects/app-3.jpg"]
Leave images: [] to show the app's initials instead.
Only use screenshots you're allowed to share (no real customer data).
