About me

Mobile engineer who takes products from planning to production.

I'm a Mobile Application Engineer with 5+ years of experience building production-grade native Android, iOS and cross-platform apps with Flutter and Kotlin Multiplatform. I've worked on consumer apps, freelance products and personal projects, as well as large-scale banking apps with real-time payments and strict security requirements. That experience carries over to any product where reliability matters, from chat and booking apps to e-commerce and on-demand services.

I care about clean architecture, offline-first design and reliable API integration. I'm comfortable owning a module end-to-end — from technical decisions and UI implementation through testing, store release and production support — and working directly with clients and stakeholders to turn requirements into shipped features.

Alongside mobile work, I've been building backend skills in Java, Kotlin, Spring Boot and Ktor through courses and personal projects, so I understand both sides of the APIs my apps depend on.

I work closely with designers, backend engineers, QA and product teams, with a steady focus on security, performance, reliability and maintainable code.
